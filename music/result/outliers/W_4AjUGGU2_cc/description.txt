Wake up with a calmer mind, a steadier breath, and a more peaceful beginning.

Morning Cortisol Detox is a soothing Indian classical meditation journey featuring the timeless beauty of Raag Bhairavi, performed on the bansuri and presented in a peaceful 432 Hz listening experience.

The first hour after waking naturally brings the highest cortisol levels of the day. While this response helps prepare the body for activity, stress and anxiety can amplify it, leaving the mind restless before the day has even begun.

Inspired by centuries of Indian classical tradition, this gentle bansuri meditation creates a calm environment for mindfulness, emotional balance, and peaceful mornings.

Whether you're practicing meditation, pranayama, yoga, journaling, reading, or simply taking a quiet moment before work, allow the flowing bansuri melodies to support a slower, more intentional start to your day.
