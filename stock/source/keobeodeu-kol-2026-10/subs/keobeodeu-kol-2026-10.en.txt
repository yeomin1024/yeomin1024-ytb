-구조
Why such a big gap?
Let's see how a covered call makes its payouts.
A covered call ETF holds stocks and sells the right to buy them at a set price.
The money from selling that right comes in monthly and funds the payouts.
But if the price rises above the set price, the gain above it goes to the buyer of the right.
On the way down, it falls less only by the money received, so nearly as much.
We checked whether that's true using daily moves.
QQQ rose more than 1% in a day on 595 days.
On those days QQQ rose 1.84% on average; QYLD rose 0.95%, keeping just 51.3%.
QQQ fell more than 1% in a day on 498 days.
On those days QQQ fell 2.04% on average and QYLD 1.28%, a full 62.7% of it.
