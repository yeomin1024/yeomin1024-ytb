The Sacred Names Within This Song:

🌊 Gaṅgādhārāya :  He who bears the Ganga. When the celestial river descended with cosmic force, Shiva caught her in his matted locks to protect all of creation from annihilation

🕉️ Rāmeśvarāya : The Lord worshipped by Rama. Even the great Rama bowed before Shiva at Rameswaram, reminding us that true devotion transcends all ego.

🌀 Jaṭādhārāya : He who wears the matted locks.The Jata symbolizes the ascetic who has renounced the world yet holds the entire cosmos within.

🌙 Someśvarāya : Lord of the Moon,Shiva who wears the crescent moon, governing time, tides, and the cycles of the mind.

🌍 Viśveśvarāya : Lord of the entire Universe, the presiding deity of Kashi, before whom all karma dissolves and liberation becomes possible.

✨ Koṭeśvarāya: Lord of countless millions. He who is the refuge of every soul across all ages, all worlds, all births
