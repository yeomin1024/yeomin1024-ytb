Should you stop your SIP when the stock market crashes? Gajendra Kothari shares his exact 15-20-25 strategy for market falls.

In this episode of Mutual Funds by PhonePe, CFA and market veteran Gajendra Kothari explains why investor behaviour — not fund selection — decides whether you build wealth.
