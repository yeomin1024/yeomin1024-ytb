The Navkar Mantra (Lyrics):
ॐ नमो अरिहंताणं (Om Namo Arihantanam)
ॐ नमो सिद्धाणं (Om Namo Siddhanam)
ॐ नमो आयरियाणं (Om Namo Ayariyanam)
ॐ नमो उवज्झायाणं (Om Namo Uvajjhayanam)
ॐ नमो लोए सव्व साहूणं (Om Namo Loe Savva Sahunam)
एसो पंच नमुक्कारो, सव्व पावप्पणासणो (Eso Panch Namukkaro, Savva Pavappanasano)
मंगलाणं च सव्वसिं, पढमं हवइ मंगलं (Mangalanam Cha Savvesim, Padhamam Havai Mangalam)

🙏 Meaning:
I bow to the Arihants (Conquerors), Siddhas (Liberated Souls), Acharyas (Spiritual Leaders), Upadhyayas (Teachers), and all Sadhus (Monks) in the world. This five-fold bow destroys all sins and is the foremost among all auspicious things.
