Raag Bhairavi — performed at dawn for centuries — was composed precisely for this moment. Its all-komal (flat) notes create a sound world of surrender and release, gently signalling to the nervous system that the day does not have to begin in tension. Paired with 432Hz tuning and the breath-like phrasing of the bansuri, this 20-minute session is designed to lower cortisol, quiet the mind, and begin your morning from a place of calm clarity.

🎵 PERFECT FOR:
• Morning meditation & pranayama
• Lowering morning stress & anxiety
• Cortisol reset before work
• Yoga & breathwork
• Quiet mornings before the day begins
• Nervous system calm & grounding

🎵 WHY RAAG BHAIRAVI HEALS:
Bhairavi's komal swaras activate the parasympathetic nervous system, slowing the breath and reducing cortisol naturally. The bansuri's long meend (glides) mirror the body's own exhale — each phrase an invitation to let go.

🎧 HOW TO USE:
1. Headphones at low, comfortable volume
2. Seated or lying down, early morning
3. Stay for the full 20 minutes — the shift is cumulative
