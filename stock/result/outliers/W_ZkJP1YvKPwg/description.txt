AI stocks are crashing while much of the market is green. Is this a buying opportunity or are some stocks still too expensive after the selloff?

Today, I compare eight AI and semiconductor stocks using earnings estimates, free cash flow and discounted cash flow valuations, before revealing the three I’d buy first.

Stocks covered:
• Nvidia (NVDA)
• Palantir (PLTR)
• Sandisk (SNDK)
• ASML (ASML)
• Micron (MU)
• Marvell (MRVL)
• Taiwan Semiconductor (TSM)
• Broadcom (AVGO)

We’ll look at why AI suppliers are falling while some of their biggest customers rally, the catch behind exceptionally low earnings multiples, and how different growth assumptions change what these stocks could be worth.

A falling share price doesn’t automatically make a stock cheap. The question is how much future growth you’re already paying for and what happens if the forecasts disappoint.
