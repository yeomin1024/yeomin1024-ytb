Why Is The Market Collapsing? 📉🚨

Indian markets are under pressure, with large-caps, mid-caps and small-caps falling together as investor sentiment weakens.
What this means 👇

✅ Nifty fell over 5% in September
✅ PSU Banks faced sharper selling
✅ Crude oil prices surged significantly
✅ Rising inflation can pressure bond yields
✅ FIIs have continued selling Indian equities  

Big takeaway:
Market corrections rarely have just one trigger. Rising crude, inflation concerns, higher yields and foreign investor outflows can collectively put pressure on equities.

Volatility can test patience but understanding why markets are moving matters more than reacting emotionally.
