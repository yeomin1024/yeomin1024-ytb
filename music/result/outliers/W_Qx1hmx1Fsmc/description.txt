My epic orchestral cover of "Odysseus" from Christopher Nolan's The Odyssey, composed by Ludwig Göransson. 

This started as a simple recreation because I wanted to understand the music and its layers, the more I worked on it, the more I felt like making a video out of it. So here it is, Odysseus + Ithaca into one epic arrangement!
