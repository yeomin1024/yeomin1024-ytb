Presenting “JAADU” by Filmy & Rawme Hooda — the first chapter of AMOR, Filmy’s 5-track EP presented by Real Music.

JAADU is a fresh Haryanvi romantic song blending Filmy’s distinctive sound with Rawme Hooda’s poetry and presence. With soulful melodies, modern production and an expressive visual story, the song captures the attraction, chemistry and emotion of falling for someone.

Shot against the striking landscapes of Azerbaijan, the official video brings a cinematic dimension to JAADU and sets the tone for the journey of AMOR.

JAADU marks the beginning. Four more songs. Four more stories.

Listen, share and experience the first chapter of AMOR.
