Line-by-Line Meaning
Karpooragauram: The one who is as pure and white as camphor.
Karunaavataaram: The personification or incarnation of compassion.
Sansaarasaram: The essence of the entire world and universe.
Bhujagendrahaaram: The one who wears the king of serpents as a garland (representing control over death and fear).
Sadaavasantam: The one who resides eternally.
Hridaayaravinde: Within the lotus of the heart.
Bhavam: Lord Shiva (the source of existence).
Bhavaaneesahitam: Together with Goddess Bhavani (Mother Parvati).
Namaami: I bow down or offer my salutations.
