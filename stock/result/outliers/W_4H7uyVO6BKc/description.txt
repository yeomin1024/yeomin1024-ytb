On this weeks episode of The Money Mindset, I sat down with Professor Aswath Damodaran, one of the most respected voices in finance and the person many call the “Dean of Valuation.” A Professor of Finance at NYU’s Stern School of Business, he has taught generations of students and investors how to answer a deceptively simple question: what is something actually worth?

Born in Chennai and an alumnus of IIM Ahmedabad, Professor Damodaran went on to earn his PhD at UCLA and become the author of widely used books on valuation and corporate finance, including Investment Valuation and The Little Book of Valuation. 
Through his blog, Musings on Markets, and his freely shared datasets and classes, he has made rigorous investing knowledge accessible to anyone willing to learn.

We talk about how to separate price from value, why stories matter as much as numbers, and how investors can stay rational when markets are anything but.
