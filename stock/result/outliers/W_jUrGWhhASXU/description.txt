In this episode of Wealth by Motilal Oswal, we are joined by Pankaj Tibrewal, Founder of Ikigai Asset Manager and one of India’s most respected fund managers with over two decades of market experience.

In a volatile market environment where investors' patience is being tested, Pankaj breaks down his core investment frameworks, his insights on reading current market conditions, managing risk, and spotting long-term wealth creators. 

Key Takeaways:

Understanding the disconnect between market performance and strong economic indicators.
How global interest rates, US bond yields, and dollar devaluation affect long-term cost of capital.
Impact of foreign investor selling and the cushion provided by domestic savings.
Evaluating business management, succession planning, and promoter-led companies.
Criteria for identifying multibaggers, including cash flows, balance sheet strength, and market size.
Balancing company growth with stock valuation using the PEG ratio.
Key growth sectors: manufacturing (Europe + 1), consumer discretionary (premiumization), and financial services.
Active vs. passive investing approaches for mid-cap and small-cap stocks.
Developing investor discipline, controlling emotions, managing risk, and the benefits of meditation.
