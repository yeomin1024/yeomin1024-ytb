No real plan, just a day trip to Buyeo — and somehow we ended up face-to-face with
the golden incense burner from every Korean history textbook 🍂
From the Baekje-era stone pagoda at Jeongnimsa Temple to a boat ride on the Baengma River
and a stroll through Gungnamji Pond, we packed 1,500 years of Baekje history into one day.

Along the way, a real tour guide taught us why the pagoda has 5 (not an even number of) floors,
why a phoenix sits on top of the incense burner, and the dramatic true story of how it was
buried underground for 1,400 years before being discovered in 1993.

The real reason we came? One month from this trip, Buyeo hosts its biggest festival of the year —
the 72nd Baekje Cultural Festival. Everything we experienced today is a preview of what's waiting
for visitors this October!
