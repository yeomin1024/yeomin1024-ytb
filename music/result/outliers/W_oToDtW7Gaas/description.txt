A piano music collection inspired by Amélie, created for reading, studying, writing, quiet work, and cozy days.

Amélie is a story about a young woman who notices the little things others often miss — a stranger’s forgotten treasure, a quiet café, a familiar street, and the small opportunities we have to make someone’s day a little brighter.

Watching her wander through Paris reminds us that happiness does not always arrive as something extraordinary. It is often hidden in the ordinary things we choose to notice: fresh bread from the bakery, flowers after the rain, a Metro ride across the city, fruit from the market, or a quiet evening at home.

That is part of growing up, too — learning that wonder does not disappear. We simply have to keep noticing it.

May this music accompany your own slow weekend, wherever you are.
