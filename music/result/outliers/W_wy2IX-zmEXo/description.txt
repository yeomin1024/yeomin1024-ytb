🌕 An ancient moon

Under a burning moon, two silhouettes meet in silence: a woman, a horned presence, and the fragile distance between desire, danger, and darkness.

This dark neoclassical ambient piece was created for slow listening, reading, writing, dreaming, or entering a nocturnal fantasy world.
Deep cello textures, shadowed atmosphere, restrained tension, and ritual elegance shape a soundscape that feels ancient, magnetic, and haunted.

No battle. No heroism.
Only the moon, the silence, and the presence waiting in the dark.

🕯️ For lovers of dark ambient, dark neoclassical music, gothic fantasy, ritual atmospheres, cello textures, and melancholic cinematic soundscapes.

🎧 Best listened to with headphones.
