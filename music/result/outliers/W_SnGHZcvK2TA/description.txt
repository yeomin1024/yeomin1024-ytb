BORN AGAIN
Is out, https://djhabias.lnk.to/BornAgain

I'm releasing my new EP project titled “Born Again.”
An episode full of 5 musical tracks related to the electronic genre, specifically Afro House.
The name “Born Again,” meaning “Born Again,” is due to the fact that lately I've been exploring a path more inclined towards Afro House and other subgenres.
For this project, Habias collaborated with artists such as Filho Do Zua, Man Beza, DJ Godiamo, and DJ & producer Track ID. Mixed and mastered by DJ Over12.
The EP is officially available on all digital platforms.
