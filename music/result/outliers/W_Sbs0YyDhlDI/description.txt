Honest Work by Jaxson Blue 🎙️

I knew a man once. Worked his whole life with his hands — the kind of work that leaves something behind but never makes you rich. People would look at him a certain way, like the dirt on his clothes was something to be sorry about.
But that man raised his children. He built things that are still standing. And when he laid his head down at night, he slept easy — because he never took what wasn't his, and he never had to lie about how he earned his bread.
I wrote this song for him. For every man and woman who built their life one honest day at a time, and never got a thank you for it.
There's no shame in a tired back and clean hands. That's the whole song, really. It was honest work — and that was always enough.
