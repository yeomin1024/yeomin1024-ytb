The stock market is in crash mode as the AI bubble collapses, and Gareth Soloway breaks down the technical levels on the S&P 500, the Nasdaq and the hardest-hit AI and chip stocks after the S&P 500 fell 4.36% on Thursday, Nov. 5, 2026, its worst day since the April 2025 tariff selloff.

Gareth explains why panic selling by retail investors is how bottoms form, why the pendulum has swung from extreme greed to extreme fear on the AI trade after the midterms, and why he started buying aggressively into the flush.

On the S&P 500, the index closed below the key level he flagged in recent videos. Gareth then walks through the support zones on Seagate (STX), SanDisk (SNDK), Marvell (MRVL), Western Digital (WDC), Applied Materials (AMAT), AMD, Broadcom (AVGO) and NVIDIA (NVDA), including gap fills, pivot lows and an RSI below 25 on Seagate.
