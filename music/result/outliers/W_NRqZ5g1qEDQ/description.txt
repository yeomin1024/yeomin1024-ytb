A Cinematic Night Drive through the heart of Tokyo and Yokohama.

Cruising through the city lights with a soulful Lo-fi Jazz Groove soundtrack.
We start on the Shuto Expressway Route 3, catching a close-up view of Tokyo Tower before crossing the iconic Rainbow Bridge. The journey continues through the stunning skyscrapers of Toyosu and Harumi, heading into the glittering streets of Ginza.

After exploring the urban vibes of Hibiya, Aoyama, and Shibuya, we jump back onto the Shuto Route 3 toward the Tomei Expressway. The final stretch takes us through the legendary Daikoku JCT, ending with a breathtaking cruise across the Yokohama Bay Bridge.

Sit back, relax, and immerse yourself in the ultimate Tokyo night vibes.
