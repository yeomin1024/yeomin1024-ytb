Gold has always been more than just an investment for Indians. It is wealth, security, tradition, emotion, and sometimes even a financial lifeline.

But is buying gold actually a good investment? Why do Indians own so much gold, and if you do want to buy it, which form makes the most sense: jewellery, physical gold, digital gold, mutual funds, or Gold ETFs?

In this video, we look beyond the usual advice and break down the real story of gold. From a ₹1 lakh gold experiment and the hidden costs of jewellery to gold's long-term returns, inflation, portfolio protection, and the reasons behind India's obsession with gold.

We also explore how gold played a crucial role during India's 1991 economic crisis and helped give the country the breathing room it needed to begin a major economic transformation.

By the end, you'll have a clearer idea of whether you should buy gold, how you should buy it, and what role gold should actually play in your financial life.
