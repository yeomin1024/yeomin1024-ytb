-언제 매수
When you're unsure when to buy, people say following buy signals raises your odds of success.
To check, we ran a backtest with AI-written Python code on real past prices.
We tested SPY, an ETF that tracks the S&P 500, from 2016.
We used 10 buy signals, like the 20-day average, RSI and the fear index VIX.
Up 5% from the buy price first counted as success; down 2% first, as failure.
The comparison: 2,000 accounts that bought on random dates.
If signals are useful, they should beat random on success rate.
Random accounts were judged by the same rule.
The best was buying when price crosses the 20-day average: a 52.1% success rate.
But all 10 were in a range no different from random buying.
