Is the Indian stock market sending mixed signals? Global investors have been cautious, domestic investors are putting in record SIP money, and company earnings are strong. 
In this podcast, Manish Gunwani, CIO of Bandhan AMC, decodes this contradiction, shares how he thinks about market cycles and returns, and explains how a regular investor can make sense of today’s market.

About the Guest: Manish Gunwani is the Chief Investment Officer of Bandhan AMC, with 25-30 years of experience in the markets. He has seen several market cycles up close, including the dot-com bust, the 2008 crisis and the Covid crash, as an analyst and as a fund manager.

This podcast reflects the guest’s personal views and experience, and is shared for informational and educational purposes only. It is not investment advice or a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security, sector, or fund. Past performance is not indicative of future results.

We also discuss:
➡️ Why foreign investors are cautious on India while domestic investors keep showing trust
➡️ Why small caps have a role in the next phase of growth, and what to look for in a small cap fund
➡️ How he decides when a stock has gone wrong, and why entry and exit matter equally
➡️ Themes he is watching over the next 10-20 years: manufacturing exporters, premium consumption and technology platforms
➡️ How to realistically think about building ₹1 crore over 10 years
➡️ Lessons from the dot-com bust, 2008 and Covid, and why asset allocation discipline matters
➡️ Why being a little contrarian helps in asset allocation
➡️ Rapid-fire round: Nifty 50 vs Nifty 500, the most underestimated sector, the book he recommends and more
