Invest in stocks with this one thought, and you're 99% sure to lose money.
It's the thought: "I want to make money fast in stocks."
What? Isn't making money the whole point of investing?
When you're out to make money, greed is bound to creep in.
When you profit, you want more, so you keep buying in bigger amounts,
and when you lose, you want it back fast, so you buy and sell more often and in bigger sizes.
Then when a big drop comes, the money to buy more is already gone, and selling means a huge loss.
At that point, there's nothing you can do but watch your account fall.
As the saying goes, haste makes waste,
so let go of greed, prepare for risk and profit slowly, and one day you'll find yourself a winner.
