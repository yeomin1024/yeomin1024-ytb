Is a stock market crash about to unfold this October? With the Nasdaq dropping as much as 4.5% last week and social media calling for a crash on Monday, Gareth Soloway, Chief Market Strategist at VerifiedInvesting.com, deep dives into the technical analysis and data to see whether the charts actually support it.

Gareth starts with October's long history as the month that breaks markets, from 1929 to Black Monday in 1987 to the 2008 financial crisis. Then he goes straight to the Nasdaq and the AI trade. CoreWeave (CRWV) has fallen about 35% in roughly ten trading days, and Gareth explains why soaring credit default swap costs on its debt have the market spooked about the entire AI build-out.

His call is contrarian: he does not buy the October crash. Gareth explains why crashes rarely come when retail expects them, why the Plunge Protection Team will be on high alert with the midterm elections two weeks away, and why crashes tend to come at the end of a decline, not the early stages. He maps the exact CoreWeave levels where he would be a buyer on a gap down, breaks down Micron's long-term breakdown and SanDisk's support, and shows why he thinks the Nasdaq may have already put in its multi-year high while the S&P 500 still has room to push toward 8,000.

He closes with his game plan for Monday, the key Nasdaq and S&P 500 support levels to watch, his new short position in oil, and whether gold and silver can hold key support this week.
