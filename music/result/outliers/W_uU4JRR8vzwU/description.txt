Times Music presents Long Way From Home by MC SQUARE, Radhika Bhide, and Wicked Sunny, a story of voices chasing their dreams. The dreams haven't shown up yet. Home feels too far to return to. Three voices writing from the same place. Too tired to move forward, too lost to turn back. This is the poetry that came out of that silence.

Long Way From Home is for anyone caught between where they came from and where they're trying to go. MC SQUARE, Radhika Bhide, and Wicked Sunny each take a verse on the track, and somehow they're all telling the same story. Don't be surprised if it lands a little too close to home.
