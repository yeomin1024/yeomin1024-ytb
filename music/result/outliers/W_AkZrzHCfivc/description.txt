A peaceful selection of 15 works by Frédéric Chopin, carefully chosen to reflect the atmosphere of autumn nights.

Rather than simply gathering familiar pieces, this collection was arranged as a journey through an autumn evening — beginning with quiet solitude, moving through rain, falling leaves and memories, reaching a deeper emotional peak, and finally settling into the stillness of a late-autumn night.

The program brings together nocturnes, preludes, waltzes, a mazurka, Étude Op.10 No.3, the lyrical Romanze from Piano Concerto No.1, and the gentle Berceuse.

The music has been gently adjusted to 432Hz to create a softer and warmer listening atmosphere.

Ideal for slow autumn evenings, reading, reflection, relaxation, studying, or simply spending a quiet moment at the end of the day.

🎧 Best enjoyed with headphones or at a comfortable low volume.
