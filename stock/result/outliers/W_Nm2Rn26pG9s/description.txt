Will the government cut LTCG and STT taxes for stock market investors? Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman’s recent remarks at a Bloomberg event have sparked fresh hopes of tax relief, as the Sensex surged over 1,100 points. In this episode of Kharcha Pani, we explain what Long-Term Capital Gains Tax (LTCG) and Securities Transaction Tax (STT) are, their current rates, and why investors have been demanding their removal for years. Why was STT introduced in 2004 when LTCG was abolished, and why are investors paying both taxes today? We also examine SBI Research’s proposal to remove LTCG tax, massive FII/FPI selling from Indian equities, and why foreign investors are pulling money out despite India's strong GDP growth. Could reducing these taxes bring foreign investment back, revive the Indian stock market, and benefit retail investors? With Union Budget 2027 preparations underway, we explore whether the Modi government could announce a major stock market tax cut and what it would mean for your investments, mutual funds, and savings.
