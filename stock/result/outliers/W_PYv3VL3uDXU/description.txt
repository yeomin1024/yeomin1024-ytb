Union Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman has signaled that the government is open to reviewing Long-Term Capital Gains (LTCG) tax and Securities Transaction Tax (STT) ahead of Union Budget 2027. Following a massive ₹3.04 Lakh Crore Foreign Institutional Investor (FII/FPI) outflow in 2026 and a blockbuster SBI Research Report recommending the abolition of LTCG tax on equities, Dalal Street witnessed a massive bull run with the BSE Sensex surging 1,109 points and Nifty 50 jumping 359 points.

- Nirmala Sitharaman’s Big Signal Before Budget 2027: What Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman said at the Bloomberg New Economy Forum in New Delhi regarding the "healthy debate" on STT and LTCG double taxation.
- Dalal Street Bull Run & Market Reaction: How a single hint on capital gains tax review triggered a massive stock market rally, boosting Sensex by 1,109 points and Nifty by 359 points despite crude oil and geopolitical tensions.
- What is STT, STCG & LTCG Tax? (Tax Checkpoints Explained): A simple breakdown of Securities Transaction Tax (0.10% on Equity Delivery, 0.05% on Futures, 0.15% on Options), Short-Term Capital Gains Tax (20%), Long-Term Capital Gains Tax (12.5% above ₹1.25 Lakh), Stamp Duty, SEBI charges, and 18% GST on brokerage.

The History of "Double Taxation" (2004 to 2026 Timeline):
- 2004 Budget (P. Chidambaram): LTCG abolished (0%) in exchange for introducing STT.
- 2018 Budget (Arun Jaitley): The return of 10% LTCG tax without removing STT.
- 2024 & 2026 Budgets (Nirmala Sitharaman): LTCG hiked from 10% to 12.5%, STCG hiked from 15% to 20%, and F&O STT hiked in April 2026.

- Why Foreign Investors (FIIs/FPIs) Are Leaving India: How rupee depreciation (3–4% annually) combined with high Capital Gains Tax and STT is hurting Post-Tax Dollar Returns, leading to a record ₹3.04 Lakh Crore FII outflow in 2026 (nearly double the ₹1.66 Lakh Crore outflow in 2025).
- Global Capital Gains Tax Comparison: Why global financial hubs like Singapore (0%), Hong Kong (0%), and UAE (0%) are attracting foreign capital while Indian equities face heavy taxation.
- SBI Research Report’s Big Warning to Govt: Why State Bank of India (SBI) recommended 0% LTCG tax on equity investments while extending the long-term holding period from 1 year to 2–3 years.
-  Government’s ₹73,700 Crore STT Jackpot: How the government’s STT collection jumped from ₹53,296 Crore (FY25) to ₹57,522 Crore (FY26), surging 53% to ₹40,214 Crore in just the first 6 months of FY27—alongside ₹1.25 Lakh Crore from Capital Gains Tax.
- Union Budget 2027 Verdict & 2 Key Options: Will the government implement the SBI formula (0% LTCG for 2–3 year holdings) or scrap the 0.10% STT on delivery trades for retail and SIP investors?
