Why are strong business results failing to lift stock prices, and where could the next opportunities be hiding?

This Diwali, Ishmohit and Naman sit down to discuss the market’s mood, the themes they’re researching and the businesses that could emerge stronger from today’s uncertainty.

We begin with the gap between earnings and sentiment. Why have investors become so impatient? How does leverage amplify volatility around results? And what can earlier market cycles teach us about periods when pessimism seems impossible to shake?

The conversation then moves into the businesses behind the themes. We explore how India’s oil dependence could accelerate battery storage and electrification, where opportunities may lie across healthcare and CDMOs, and what needs to change for unpopular sectors such as consumer EMS and recycling.

A recurring question runs through it all: where is demand growing faster than supply can respond? From power components and specialised manufacturing to AI infrastructure, we look at bottlenecks, barriers to entry and companies moving into higher-value businesses. We also discuss where valuations or business risks make us more cautious.

The aim is to share a way of thinking you can apply beyond this Diwali: connect an industry tailwind to a specific business, understand what is changing, and examine the risks before forming a view.
