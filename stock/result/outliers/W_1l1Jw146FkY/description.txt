Brazil’s financial markets have crashed following a shock presidential election result. The country’s main stock market index plunged, the Brazilian real fell sharply against the US dollar, and the sell-off was so severe that trading had to be suspended.

President Lula has secured another term in office, but investors are concerned about Brazil’s rising public debt, high interest rates and future government spending. The turmoil has also hit Brazilian government bonds, prompting the Treasury to cancel two planned debt auctions.

In this video, I look at what caused the market crash, why investors reacted so dramatically and what the result could mean for Brazil’s economy.
