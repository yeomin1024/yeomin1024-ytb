Why are foreign investors pulling billions out of the Indian stock market even as India’s economy continues to grow? In October 2026, foreign institutional investors (FIIs) have withdrawn over ₹40,000 crore from Indian equities in just a handful of trading sessions, intensifying a sell-off that began months earlier.

In this episode of The Indian Express, Sayoni Aiyar explains the three major global forces driving the exodus: rising US Treasury yields, a strengthening US dollar and surging crude oil prices. We also examine how currency depreciation, import costs and India’s tax structure are affecting foreign investors’ returns, and why domestic institutions and mutual funds are helping absorb the selling pressure.

If India’s economic fundamentals remain resilient, why is foreign capital heading for the exit? And what does this mean for Indian markets and ordinary investors?
