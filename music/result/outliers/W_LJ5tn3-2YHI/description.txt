In this performance from the documentary film YOSHIKI: UNDER THE SKY, YOSHIKI plays “ENDLESS RAIN” on his crystal piano as fans from around the world join him for the iconic chorus.

Filmed beneath the downtown Los Angeles skyline, the performance also features archival footage of hide and X JAPAN from 1998. This moment holds profound personal meaning for YOSHIKI, as his mother passed away shortly after the performance was filmed.
