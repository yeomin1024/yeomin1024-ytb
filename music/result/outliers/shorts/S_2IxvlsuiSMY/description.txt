🌌 Om Mani Padme Hum · Cosmic Buddhist Mandala of Light

Om Mani Padme Hum is the sacred six-syllable mantra of Avalokiteśvara,
embodying boundless compassion and wisdom,
and symbolizing the primordial vibration of the universe
and the awakening of the mind.

This meditation,
like a luminous Buddhist mandala blooming within infinite cosmic space,
gently purifies confused thoughts and inner afflictions,
awakening deep stillness and compassion within the heart.

The mandala, unfolding in perfect circular harmony,
represents the order and balance of the universe
and the path toward enlightenment,
while light flows as an energy of compassion, healing, and awakening,
embracing all beings without distinction.

Within this meditation of sound and light,
the mind becomes one with the cosmos,
and pure awareness, like a lotus in bloom,
quietly arises from within.
