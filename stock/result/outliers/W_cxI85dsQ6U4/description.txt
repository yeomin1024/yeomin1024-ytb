In this episode of Stock Market ka Tamasha, we have Five SEBI cases. Five stories every retail investor and trader in Indian stock markets needs to know. 

This month: a ponzi scheme hidden inside an agri-investment platform, an Adani MPS investigation that ran for 6 years and closed for Rs 1.48 crore, a market structure experiment reversed in 40 days, a US algo trading firm challenging SEBI in SAT, and a new settlement regulation that is less a reform and more a published rate card for market violations.

1. GROWPITAL PONZI SCHEME | Rs 192 Crore Agri Investment Fraud

Growpital promised 10 to 18% guaranteed returns on jeera farming investments in Barmer, Rajasthan.

- 5,208 investors put in a total of Rs 192 crore between April 2020 and June 2023
- SEBI’s investigation team reached Barmer and found fake companies, fake invoices, and zero actual farming activity-
- Three people arrested - Rituraj Sharma (face of the scheme), Gayatri Rinwa, and Krishna Joshi
- Rs 50 crore frozen out of Rs 192 crore; accused claimed Rs 130 crore was spent on the business
- SEBI penalty on a Rs 192 crore fraud: Rs 6 crore

2. ADANI MPS CASE | Rs 1,984 Crore Profit, Rs 1.48 Crore Settlement

SEBI alleged that two FPIs linked to Vinod Adani held shares in Adani Enterprises, Adani Ports, ACC, and Adani Transmission between 2013 and 2018, breaching the 75% promoter shareholding limit:

- The arrangement allegedly generated unlawful profits of Rs 1,984 crore across the four entities
- After 6 years of investigation, SEBI could not establish a direct communication link between Vinod Adani and the FPIs
- Rs 1,984 crore was let go - no disgorgement, no recovery order
- Four Adani companies settled for a combined Rs 1.48 crore - Rs 37.5 lakh per company - without admitting or denying the allegations

3. CAS REVERSAL | SEBI’s Closing Auction Session Rolled Back in 40 Days

SEBI launched the Closing Auction Session (CAS) on August 3, 2026 - a 10-minute auction window before market close to set the closing price:
- On F&O expiry days, index values swung 2% or more inside the CAS window before recovering - retail traders settled contracts at artificial prices
- SEBI itself issued manipulation orders against two companies for CAS-related price rigging within weeks of the launch
- 20,000+ market participants responded to SEBI’s September 12 consultation paper - traders, brokers, and fund houses all demanded a rollback on F&O settlement
- On October 10, SEBI Chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey confirmed that F&O contract settlement will return to the 30-minute VWAP model within a week

4. JANE STREET VS SEBI | SAT Hearing, Rs 4,843 Crore at Stake

SEBI alleges that Jane Street - a US algorithmic trading firm - manipulated India’s index options market by moving Nifty and Bank Nifty prices on expiry days through large futures and equity positions, then profiting in options
- Alleged unlawful profit: Rs 4,843 crore
- SEBI issued an ex-parte interim order in July 2026, freezing the full amount without giving Jane Street a prior hearing 
- Jane Street challenged the order in SAT; central argument: SEBI’s detailed investigation report was completed after the order date - the judgment was passed before the analysis was finished
- SAT final order expected on October 21, 2026

5. SEBI SETTLEMENT REGULATIONS 2026 | Formula-Based Rate Card for Market Violations

SEBI board approved the Settlement Regulations 2026 on September 24, 2026

- Formula-based calculation introduced: Settlement Amount = Base Amount x (Stage Factor + Risk Factor + Gravity Factor + Applicant Factor - Mitigating Factor)
- Every category of entity now has a pre-calculable settlement range before a case even begins
- Fast-track route introduced for cases up to Rs 10 lakh
- New pre-show-cause notice window: 60 days to settle before SEBI formally issues a notice - investigations can close before they are complete
