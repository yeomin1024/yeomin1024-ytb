DELTARUNE Piano Collections, Vol. 2 is coming to all streaming platforms Oct. 30!

If Vol. 1 was my precious baby lamb, Vol. 2 is my rebellious teenager. The arrangements are even more unhinged and feature Chapters 3 and 4 prominently (with a few Ch. 1 and 2 gems sprinkled in).

This time around, we stepped up our game and recorded at Ocean Way Nashville, one of the greatest studios in the world (Octopath Traveler 2, God of War, God of War Ragnarok, Destiny 2... I am kidding you not) on their gorgeous Yamaha C7 in a *literal sanctuary* (appropriately 😉).

We made... quite a few... music videos and this is one of them! Enjoy the full official music video of It's TV Time!, my personal favorite arrangement from the album.

I can't wait for you all to hear what I spent the past year working on and I hope you enjoy this first look!
