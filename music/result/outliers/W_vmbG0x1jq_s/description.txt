Every pianist has a list of melodies that linger in their minds. Here are my 10 most beautiful melodies ever written for the piano. Choosing only 10 feels impossible when composers like Chopin or Rachmaninoff could easily fill a list of their own. For this collection, I selected the ones that move me the most.

I would love to hear which melodies hold that same power for you!
