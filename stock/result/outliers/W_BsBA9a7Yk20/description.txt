Ed Elson is joined by Catherine Rampell to break down the key takeaways from the midterm results, whether Trump will change course on tariffs and Iran, and what the results mean for the future of the MAGA movement. Catherine Rampell is an economics editor at The Bulwark, host of the Receipts Podcast, and anchor at MS NOW. Alex Bores is a New York State Assemblymember, author of the RAISE Act, and founder of Who Decides.
