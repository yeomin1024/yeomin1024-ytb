Zindagi | Zindagi
by cefur | studio

A dark cinematic Urdu song about pain, punishment, survival, and the cruel voice of life saying:

“Chal thora aur, Zindagi.”

Zindagi is for the ones who kept walking even after life stopped feeling kind.

It carries loneliness, regret, exhaustion, and that strange punishment of still being alive when everything inside you already feels burned.

Opening couplet by Firaq Gorakhpuri:

“Kam se kam maut se aisi mujhe umeed nahi
Zindagi tu ne to dhoke pe diya hai dhoka”
