Dividend Stocks vs. Growth Stocks: The Math Most Investors Ignore

--------

Dividend stocks vs. growth stocks — but compared correctly this time. Most investors argue "4% yield" against "12% growth," and those aren't even the same kind of number. In this breakdown we run both strategies through identical math: total return, dividend reinvestment, corporate capital allocation, and taxes.

Two hypothetical $32,000 portfolios. Twenty years. One collects $54,000 in dividends. The other collects $7,700. We calculate exactly where they finish — and which single variable decides it.
