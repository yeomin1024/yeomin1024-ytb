What would you do with an extra ₹50,000 every month?

Would you invest it in stocks or mutual fund SIPs, buy gold, take a loan to buy land, or use it to prepay your home loan?

At first, the answer may seem obvious. But when you compare these four options over a 20-year period, the decision becomes much more complicated.

In this video, I break down these 4 wealth-building choices using historical data, return potential, liquidity, risk, volatility, predictability and—most importantly—the psychology required to stay invested.

You’ll understand:

• ₹50,000/month SIP: How much wealth could equity have created over 20 years?
• Large Cap vs Mid Cap vs Small Cap vs Nifty 500 vs S&P 500
• Is investing in gold every month a better option than equity?
• Gold investment: returns, liquidity and long periods of low performance
• Is buying land with a loan a smart wealth-building strategy?
• Real estate vs stocks: which has more predictable returns?
• The biggest risks of land investment—including liquidity and property-related issues
• Should you use ₹50,000/month to prepay your home loan?
• Home loan prepayment vs investing: what could you potentially gain or lose?
• How your personality, financial knowledge, risk tolerance and behaviour should influence your investment decision
• Why the “best” investment option may not be the same for everyone

The video also compares potential wealth creation using historical return assumptions across equity, gold and real estate. These historical figures are used only as a framework for comparison and do not guarantee future returns.
