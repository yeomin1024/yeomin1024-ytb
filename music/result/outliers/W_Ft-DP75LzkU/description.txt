🧠 Understanding Monaural Beats

Monaural beats are created by mixing two slightly different tones in the same audio signal (not split between left and right ears). Because the tones physically interact, the result becomes amplitude-modulated, producing a measurable rhythmic “pulsing” at the difference between the two frequencies.
