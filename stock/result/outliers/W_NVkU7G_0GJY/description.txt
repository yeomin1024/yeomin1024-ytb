Wall Street has been hit by a massive technology sell-off, with the Nasdaq 100 on course to lose approximately $2 TRILLION in market value during October as fears over America's AI spending boom intensify.

Intel plunged nearly 29% in its worst trading day since 1974, while Nvidia and AMD also suffered heavy losses. Now, investors are questioning whether America's biggest technology companies can deliver the enormous profits promised by artificial intelligence.

In this WLA report, Angus Scott examines the growing pressure on Wall Street, Amazon's soaring infrastructure costs, Microsoft's and Meta's AI investments, and why some of the world's most powerful companies are facing difficult questions about their financial future.

Is this a temporary market correction, or is Wall Street beginning to lose confidence in the trillion-dollar AI boom?
