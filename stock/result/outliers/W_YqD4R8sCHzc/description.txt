What does it take to become a professional trader in the stock market? In this episode of Trading Titans, we sit down with Ravindra Rokde, an experienced trader who shares his approach to price action trading, volume analysis, risk management, and trading psychology.

Ravindra breaks down his three-step trading strategy and explains how traders can identify high-momentum stocks by studying price movements, volume, and base formation. He also shares his unique “Trade to Lose” mindset, explaining how accepting risk before entering a trade can help reduce emotional decision-making and build discipline.

The conversation explores trading system development, win rates, risk-reward ratios, backtesting, and performance analysis. Ravindra explains why traders should focus on tested rules and practise across thousands of charts instead of constantly searching for the perfect trading strategy.
