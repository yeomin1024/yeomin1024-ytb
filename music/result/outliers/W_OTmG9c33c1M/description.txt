🌍 [English]
Doing well does not always mean running without stopping. Sometimes pausing, breathing for a moment, and allowing yourself to rest is also a form of courage. The world tells us to move faster, but a tired heart quietly says, “It’s okay to rest today.”

This song is a Korean Gugak hip-hop healing track for a heart that has been enduring for too long. It is okay not to be perfect, and it is okay to be slower than others. Resting does not mean you are falling behind. It is a moment to breathe again, to gently hold the heart that almost collapsed, and to let yourself recover. Today, you do not have to pretend to be fine. It’s okay to rest.
