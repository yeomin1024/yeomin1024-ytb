Sanskrit:
Om Bhuur-Bhuvah Svah Tat-Savitur-Varennyam 
Bhargo Devasya Dhiimahi Dhiyo Yo Nah Pracodayaat

The Gayatri Mantra is a sacred chant from the Rigveda that meditates on the glory of the solar deity Savitur, invoking him to enlighten our intellect. It encompasses the three realms of the universe—earth, atmosphere, and heaven—seeking light and wisdom.

A broad translation is:
"We meditate on the glory of the Creator's radiant light. May it inspire our intellect."
