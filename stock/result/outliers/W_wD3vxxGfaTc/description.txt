1. When Should You Start Investing?

Kirtan Shah explains why the decision to begin investing should depend on your financial situation and preparedness rather than waiting to accumulate a large amount.

The discussion compares a ₹500 SIP with a ₹5 lakh SIP and highlights the importance of developing financial discipline.

2. FD vs Mutual Funds

Understand how inflation, taxation, liquidity, risk, and potential returns affect different investment options.

The discussion also explains arbitrage funds and why they may be considered by some investors seeking alternatives to traditional savings products.

3. What Should Come Before Your SIP?

Learn why health insurance, appropriate term insurance, and an emergency fund are important components of personal financial planning.

Kirtan discusses maintaining an emergency corpus based on your household expenses and financial responsibilities.

4. NAV, Expense Ratio and Exit Load

Confused by mutual fund terminology?

This podcast simplifies Net Asset Value (NAV), Total Expense Ratio (TER), and Exit Load to help you understand how mutual funds are priced and what costs may apply.

5. SIP vs Lump Sum

Should you invest regularly through an SIP or wait until the market falls?

Kirtan discusses the challenges of market timing and the importance of following a long-term, goal-based investment process.

6. Mutual Fund Mistakes to Avoid

Learn why chasing past returns, holding too many funds, investing without understanding sector risks, and expecting guaranteed returns can create problems.

Who Should Watch This Podcast?

This episode is ideal for beginners, SIP investors, young professionals, and anyone looking to understand mutual funds before making investment decisions.
