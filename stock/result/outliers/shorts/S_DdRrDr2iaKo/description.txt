Gold prices have seen a massive rally, but can gold reach ₹4 lakh per 10 grams? Some global analysts have given extremely bullish long-term targets for gold, raising questions about how far the rally can continue.

The key drivers include rising global debt, weakening currency purchasing power, strong central bank gold purchases and the growing “debasement trade”. At the same time, gold prices can experience sharp corrections even during a broader long-term uptrend.

For investors, the focus should be on asset allocation and gradually accumulating gold through Gold ETFs or Gold Mutual Funds rather than trying to predict the perfect entry point.
