People say saving for years in TQQQ, which tracks the Nasdaq-100 at 3x, leaves far more.
To check, we ran a backtest with AI-written Python code on real past prices.
We compared QQQ, tracking the Nasdaq-100 at 1x, with 2x QLD and 3x TQQQ.
QLD and TQQQ didn't exist in 1999, so we used 2x or 3x the index each day, minus costs.
From 1999, each of the three accounts got the same $500 every month.
Even in crashes, all three kept buying every month.
By 2026, each account had put in about $170,000.
The 3x account had about $17,000,000, 8.55 times 1x's about $2,000,000.
But in 2001, the 3x account lost 92.3% of the money put in.
In 2008, it lost 89.5%, too.
