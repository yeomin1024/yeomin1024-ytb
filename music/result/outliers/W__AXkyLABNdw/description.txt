## Hare Krishna Mahamantra

**Hare Krishna Hare Krishna**
**Krishna Krishna Hare Hare**
**Hare Rama Hare Rama**
**Rama Rama Hare Hare**

The scriptures glorify the chanting of the Holy Names as the most effective spiritual practice for cultivating inner peace, devotion, and God consciousness. Whether you chant along or simply listen, these **108 Mahamantra repetitions** create a peaceful atmosphere for the heart and mind.
