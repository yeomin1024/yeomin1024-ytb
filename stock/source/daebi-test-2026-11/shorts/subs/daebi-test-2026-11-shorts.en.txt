Invest in stocks with this thought, and you'll never escape a crash.
It's believing you're good at stocks because you made money in a bull market.
You made money, so you did well. Why is that a problem?
In a bull market, most stocks rise together, so it's hard to tell skill from luck.
Of the companies in today's S&P 500, 14 rose more than 2x in 2020.
9 of them saw their stock price cut in half within 2 years.
Mistake luck for skill, and you'll bet bigger on one stock and even borrow to do it.
Then, when a crash comes, your gains and even your principal collapse all at once.
Just as ripe rice bows its head,
the more you profit, call it luck and set part of your gains aside.
Only those who go slowly and humbly like this survive a crash.
