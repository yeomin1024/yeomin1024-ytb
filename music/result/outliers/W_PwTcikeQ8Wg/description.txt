Sing along to the divine Parvati (Jab Zid Pe Aa Gayi Parvati) 🙏 — from the hit film Hanuman Ansh, out now on Junglee Music.

Sung, written and composed by Sadhu Tiwari, Parvati beautifully captures the divine energy of Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati — as the Saptarishi try to reason with Parvati about the simple, unworldly Bholenath. Based on the miraculous life of Neem Karoli Baba (Maharaj-ji), Hanuman Ansh has won hearts worldwide. Feel every word.
