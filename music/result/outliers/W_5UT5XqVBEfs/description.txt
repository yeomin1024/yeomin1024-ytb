🌙 English
Enter a sacred soundscape inspired by Divine Royal Guanyin, the Great Compassion Mantra, and the celestial dragon of protection. This music is created for prayer, meditation, relaxation, quiet reflection, and peaceful background listening.

May compassion guide the heart, calm the mind, and bring a gentle sense of blessing and inner peace.

Use headphones or speakers at a comfortable volume. Allow the music to accompany your prayer, meditation, relaxation, or quiet evening ritual.
