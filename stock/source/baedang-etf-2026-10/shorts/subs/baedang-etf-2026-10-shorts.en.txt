-SCHD vs SPY
People say saving in a dividend ETF for years leaves more than an index ETF.
To check, we ran a backtest with AI-written Python code on real past prices.
We tested SCHD, a US dividend stock ETF.
We compared it with SPY, which pays smaller dividends but tracks the S&P 500.
From 2011, each account got $1,000 every month.
In both accounts, every dividend had 15% tax withheld, then was reinvested.
15% is the rate withheld on US dividends in many countries.
By 2026, each account had put in $180,000.
The SCHD account had about $480,000; the SPY account, about $590,000.
SCHD received 2.1 times SPY's dividends, but SPY had 1.22 times as much money left.
