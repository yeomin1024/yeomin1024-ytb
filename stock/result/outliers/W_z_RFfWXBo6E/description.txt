Reliance Jio Platforms has officially filed its Red Herring Prospectus (RHP) for India’s biggest-ever Initial Public Offering (IPO) worth ₹30,213 Crore! Opening for retail subscription from October 21 to October 23, Mukesh Ambani’s Jio IPO has fixed a price band of ₹1,065 to ₹1,119 per share at a massive ₹10.3 Lakh Crore valuation. In this video, Amar Prasad decodes the complete Jio RHP filing, Grey Market Premium (GMP), Jio vs Bharti Airtel P/E valuation discount, FY26 financials, 6 future growth engines, and the hidden Data Monetization Risk every retail investor must know before applying.

Key Highlights Covered in This Video:
- India's Biggest IPO Details: Jio Platforms is issuing 27 Crore fresh equity shares to raise ₹30,213 Crore—surpassing Hyundai India (₹27,870 Cr) and NSE (₹22,562 Cr) to become the largest IPO in Indian stock market history.

- 100% Fresh Issue (Zero OFS): Neither Reliance promoters nor global investors return-selling via Offer For Sale (OFS). ₹27,500 Crore of the IPO proceeds will directly go toward debt repayment, slashing interest costs and boosting net profits.

- 25% Valuation Discount & GMP: While Jio’s estimated fair value stands at ₹11.5–₹12 Lakh Crore, the IPO is priced at ₹10.3 Lakh Crore (33.3x FY26 earnings vs Bharti Airtel’s 40x P/E), offering a 20%–25% valuation discount on the table for investors.

- Strong FY26 Financials & Free Cash Flow: Jio recorded ₹1.47 Lakh Crore in revenue (16% CAGR), 52% EBITDA margins (18% EBITDA growth), and ₹30,000 Crore in net profit, while reducing its Net Debt ratio from 0.88x to 0.36x as its massive 5G Capex cycle nears completion.

- Unmatched Market Dominance: #1 in Mobile Network (53.3 Crore subscribers & 28 Crore 5G users), #1 in Home Broadband (2.86 Crore homes), and #1 in 5G Home Wireless—commanding 49.8% wireless broadband share and 60% of India’s mobile data traffic.

- 6 Future Growth Engines: Upcoming telecom tariff hikes (with 65%–80% EBITDA flow-through), 26 Crore 2G-to-4G/5G user upgrades, rural home broadband expansion, 7.9 Crore MSME digital enterprise services, AI & Data Centers, and Satellite Internet (Satellite Constellation & Ground Stations).

- Crucial RHP Risk Factor: How India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act and TRAI’s Net Neutrality rules on 5G network slicing could impact Jio’s future data monetization plans.
