“Without You Lord, I Am Nothing” is a deeply emotional Christian worship song about surrender, grace, healing, and complete dependence on God. Featuring heartfelt female vocals, deep blending harmonies, cinematic worship melodies, soft piano, and powerful lyrics, this worship ballad reminds every broken heart that true peace and purpose can only be found in Jesus Christ.

This song is for anyone feeling lost, weary, broken, or searching for hope. No matter how far we wander, God’s mercy still reaches us. Without Him, we are empty — but with Him, we are restored, renewed, and loved forever.

✨ Lyrics inspired by surrender, faith, worship, healing, and the unfailing love of God.

Lyrics:

Without You Lord, I Am Nothing

Intro 
Sometimes we try to carry the weight of life alone…
Pretending we are strong enough… wise enough… whole enough…
But when the night grows silent,
And every mask falls to the ground,
We realize the truth…
Without His breath, we cannot sing.
Without His mercy, we cannot stand.
Without His love… we are empty.
Tonight, let every broken heart remember—
We were never meant to live without Him.
Because without You, Lord…
I am nothing.

Verse 1
(Female Lead – soft, trembling emotion)
I built my dreams on sinking sand,
Thought I could make it on my own.
I chased the lights this world could give,
But still my soul felt all alone.
Every road I walked without You
Only led me back to pain,
Then You found me in my weakness
And called my heart by name.

Pre-Chorus
(Background harmonies slowly rising)
When I was lost in the dark,
Your mercy covered me…
And when my strength fell apart,
Your arms carried me.

Chorus
(Powerful emotional release)
Without You, Lord, I am nothing,
Just a voice without a song.
A heart without a reason,
A soul that can’t go on.
You are the breath inside me,
The hope that keeps me strong.
Without You, Lord, I am nothing…
But with You, I belong.

Verse 2
(Deeper emotion, cinematic build)
You saw the tears I hid from everyone,
The battles no one knew.
Still You stayed beside my brokenness
And made my spirit new.
You turned my mourning into worship,
My fear into a flame,
Now every heartbeat longs to honor
The beauty of Your name.

Pre-Chorus
(Choir harmonies swelling)
You lifted me from ashes,
You opened blinded eyes…
Now every part of who I am
Forever testifies.

Chorus
(Higher, stronger, soaring vocals)
Without You, Lord, I am nothing,
Just a branch without the vine.
A river running empty,
A star without the light.
You are my peace in sorrow,
My shelter through the storm.
Without You, Lord, I am nothing…
In Your love I am reborn.

Bridge
Take everything, Lord…
If it brings me closer to You.
Strip away my pride,
Until all that remains is truth.
I don’t want fame,
I don’t want glory for myself…
I only want Your presence,
More than anything else.

(Choir repeats softly)
More than anything else…
More than anything else…

Final Chorus
(Full cinematic worship explosion)
Without You, Lord, I am nothing,
But Your grace has rescued me.
You turned my broken pieces
Into a melody.
Now every breath will praise You,
For all eternity…
Without You, Lord, I am nothing…
But You mean everything to me.

Outro
Without You...
I am nothing…
Jesus…
You are everything.
