Whether you're practicing meditation, pranayama, yoga, journaling, reading, preparing for work, studying or simply enjoying a quiet sunrise, allow the gentle Bansuri melodies and flowing ambient textures to accompany your morning.

🎵 MUSICAL NOTE

This composition is inspired by the emotional depth and traditional character of Raag Bhairavi, interpreted through an original ambient arrangement centered around the Bansuri.

The music combines slow Bansuri melodies with warm cinematic ambient pads, crystal singing bowls, delicate Swarmandal textures and a continuous gentle river stream to create a spacious and peaceful morning soundscape.

Artistic interpretation and melodic variations naturally occur throughout the performance.

Presented in a peaceful 432 Hz listening experience.
