🎬 ABOUT THE SOUNDTRACK & FILM

The "Love Is in the Air" theme highlights Burwell’s masterful use of the "uncanny valley" in his score. It allows the listener to experience the exact psychological shift the film portrays: a seamless descent from the expansive, nostalgic feeling of new love directly into suffocating claustrophobia and dread.

• Part 1 plays during the illusory "honeymoon phase" right after Bear's wish takes effect. It leans into dreamy, soft-focus optimism, capturing the false warmth and innocence of a wish fulfilled—though an insidious, ominous undertone already hints at the danger.

• Part 2 arrives at the film's chilling conclusion. The originally romantic melody is completely warped and distorted, reflecting the horrific, violent reality of Nikki's supernatural obsession.
