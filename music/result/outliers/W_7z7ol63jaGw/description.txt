— Veena — one of the oldest melodic instruments of South India
— Kanjira — small frame drum with bright attack and rhythmic syncopation
— Ghatam — clay pot drum, warm bass and characteristic clicks

📖 ABOUT THE CARNATIC ENSEMBLE

The combination of mridangam, kanjira, and ghatam forms the classical Carnatic rhythm ensemble — Laya Vinyasam — which has anchored South Indian classical concerts for centuries. When joined by the veena's melodic voice and the tanpura's harmonic drone, the result is a complete sonic world — rhythmic, melodic, and harmonic at once. This composition is a modern reinterpretation of that tradition, created with deep respect for the lineage.
