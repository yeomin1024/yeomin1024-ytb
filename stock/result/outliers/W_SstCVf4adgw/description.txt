US technology stocks suffer a massive sell-off as the Nasdaq plunges, wiping out an estimated $1.3 trillion in market value within just 30 minutes of trading. Semiconductor giants and AI-linked companies, including Nvidia, AMD, Qualcomm, Micron, Tesla, SanDisk and Western Digital, lead the decline, with several stocks falling by 10% or more. But what is driving the sudden Wall Street bloodbath? Investors are increasingly questioning whether the massive investments in artificial intelligence and data centre infrastructure will deliver sufficient returns. Concerns over AI regulation following the Democrats' sweeping midterm election victory, disappointing corporate outlooks and rising financing costs are adding to market uncertainty. Is this a temporary correction or an early warning that the AI investment boom is losing momentum? We examine the reasons behind the technology stock crash, the companies suffering the biggest losses and what could happen next on Wall Street.
