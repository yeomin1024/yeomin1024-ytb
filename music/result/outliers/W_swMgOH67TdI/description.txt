ABOUT THIS PERFORMANCE:
The Enthiran BGM represents some of A.R. Rahman's most ambitious large-scale orchestral scoring, built to match the epic sci-fi spectacle of Rajinikanth's Enthiran (Robot). This custom orchestral adaptation delivers a definitive live movie theme BGM experience through an 80-piece symphony orchestra.
