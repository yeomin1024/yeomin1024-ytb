-커버드콜 vs QQQ
Covered call ETFs pay out monthly, and people say spending it barely dents the principal.
To check, we ran a backtest with AI-written Python code on real past prices.
We tested QYLD, a Nasdaq-100 covered call ETF.
We compared it with QQQ, an ETF that tracks the Nasdaq-100 without covered calls.
In December 2013, each account got $100,000 of principal.
QYLD's payouts were all spent as soon as they came.
To keep it fair, the QQQ account sold the same amount on the same days.
By 2026, both accounts had paid out the same: about $110,000.
The QYLD account had about $75,000 left, 25.3% below the principal.
The QQQ account had about $510,000 left, 6.83 times QYLD.
