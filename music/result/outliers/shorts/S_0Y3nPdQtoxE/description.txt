Today I was playing piano at Gare de Lyon when something INCREDIBLE happened…

Lang Lang, the greatest classical pianist in the world, walked up to me and asked me to play Rush E, one of the hardest piano pieces ever.

What happened next… I still can’t believe.

He joined me on the second piano next to me, and we started having an epic piano battle 🔥

Since I was a child, Lang Lang was my idol.
He inspired me to start playing the piano…

I never imagined that one day I’d meet him and… challenge him!

This was a real dream come true 🥹
