This piece is inspired by the rich melodic traditions of Persian dastgah and the dynamic rhythms of Andalusian flamenco, blending cultural elements into a unified musical experience.

Experience 63 minutes of Persian–Spanish fusion instrumental music built on authentic musical traditions — expressive Persian dastgah melodies, passionate Andalusian flamenco rhythms, elegant qanun flourishes, and deep daf pulses.
